Favorite game: God of war 2018 & Ragnarök

I recently finished playing the 2018 version of God of War, also known as God of War IV. Right now, I am playing its sequel, God of War Ragnarök. These two are my favorite games because I really enjoy the storytelling and gameplay. I especially like the relationship between Kratos and his son, Atreus, because it makes the story more emotional and interesting. I also enjoy how their relationship changes and develops throughout the games.
